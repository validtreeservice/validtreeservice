Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Valid Tree Service LLC handles information when you visit our website, request an estimate, sign a contract, schedule service or make a payment.

Effective date: August 14, 2026

1. Who we are

Valid Tree Service LLC provides tree removal, tree trimming, stump grinding, land clearing and related services in Houston, Texas and surrounding communities. In this policy, “we,” “us” and “our” refer to Valid Tree Service LLC.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us and limited technical information needed to operate our website and services. This may include:

  • Contact information: your name, phone number, email address and preferred way to communicate.
  • Property and service information: the service address, project description, access information, requested service, preferred dates, and photos or videos you send for an estimate or job.
  • Business records: estimates, contracts, signatures, scheduled dates, invoices, payment amounts and payment status.
  • Communications: calls, texts, emails and other messages you exchange with us.
  • Technical information: our website host or security providers may automatically receive an IP address, browser or device type, access time, requested pages and similar server-log information.

The estimate form on our public homepage prepares a text message on your device. It does not submit the information to a website database unless and until you choose to send the text.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • respond to questions and provide estimates;
  • evaluate a property, prepare a scope of work and perform requested services;
  • prepare and manage contracts, signatures, scheduling, invoices and payments;
  • send service-related confirmations, reminders and updates;
  • maintain business, safety, insurance, accounting and legal records;
  • protect customers, workers, our systems and our legal rights; and
  • maintain and improve our website and customer experience.

4. Payments

When electronic payment options are offered, payment providers such as Stripe, Zelle, your bank or card network may process payment information under their own privacy policies. We may receive transaction details such as your name, payment amount, transaction identifier and payment status. We do not need or intend to store your complete card number or online-banking credentials.

5. When we share information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for targeted advertising. We may share information only as reasonably necessary with:

  • service providers supporting website hosting, communications, contracts, scheduling, document storage, payment processing and business operations;
  • workers, subcontractors or professional advisers who need the information to help complete or support a requested job;
  • insurers, attorneys, accountants, government agencies or law enforcement when reasonably necessary or legally required; or
  • a successor organization if our business is involved in a merger, sale or transfer, subject to appropriate protection of the information.

We require service providers to use information only for the services they provide to us, subject to their agreements and applicable law.

6. Cookies, analytics and external links

At the effective date of this policy, our public website does not use advertising pixels or company-operated analytics cookies, and we do not use website activity for targeted advertising. Our website host may use technical systems and server logs needed to deliver and secure the site.

Our website may link to third-party services such as Facebook, Google, Stripe or Zelle. When you follow an external link or use a third-party service, that provider’s privacy policy applies to its collection and use of information.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining contract and payment records, meeting tax, insurance and legal requirements, resolving disputes and enforcing agreements. Retention periods vary according to the type of record and applicable requirements. We may delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask us to confirm whether we hold personal information about you and request access, correction or deletion. You may also ask questions about how information is used or shared. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request, and we may retain information when required for contracts, payments, safety, legal obligations or other permitted business purposes.

Submit a request by emailing ValidTreeService@gmail.com or calling or texting 832-445-6535. We will not discriminate against you for making a privacy request.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No website, message, storage system or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not send Social Security numbers, banking passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

10. Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for property owners, tenants, business representatives and other adults seeking tree services. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date above. Material changes will apply only as permitted by law.
