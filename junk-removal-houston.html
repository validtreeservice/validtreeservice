Material safety

Tell us exactly what is in the load.

Paint, fuel, chemicals, oils, batteries, pressurized containers, medical waste, biohazards and other regulated materials require special handling and are not accepted unless specifically reviewed and agreed in writing. Do not hide unknown material inside bags, boxes or containers.

Junk removal questions

Before pickup day.

What kinds of junk can you remove?

We can quote furniture, household items, garage and storage cleanouts, yard debris, and approved light construction debris. Send clear photos so the material can be reviewed first.

How is the price calculated?

Pricing depends on material type, volume, weight, labor, access and disposal costs. Your written estimate identifies what is accepted and included.

Do I need to move everything outside?

Not always. Show us where the items are located, including stairs, narrow halls, gates and parking access, so the required labor can be included.

Do you accept hazardous material?

Hazardous, regulated and biohazardous material is not accepted unless it is specifically identified, reviewed and agreed to in writing before the job.
