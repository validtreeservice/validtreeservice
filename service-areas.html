Service area questions

Before you schedule.

How far does Valid Tree Service travel?

Our general service area reaches approximately 70 miles from Houston, including Cleveland and communities north, south, east and west of the city. Final availability depends on road mileage, the job scope and the schedule.

Do you serve my town if it is not listed?

Possibly. The directory covers many of the communities we serve, but it is not every neighborhood or unincorporated area. Call or text the property address for confirmation.

What should I send for a faster estimate?

Send the property address, clear photos of the trees, the work you want completed and any access concerns such as gates, fences, roofs or nearby utilities.
