The work plan

Controlled removal in a tight residential setting.

The crew first established safe work and drop zones. A climber removed limbs and trunk sections progressively, using ropes where control was needed. Ground workers consolidated brush and logs, then used compact equipment to load material without turning the yard into a heavy-equipment work zone.

  • Hazard and access evaluation
  • Climbing and controlled sectional removal
  • Protection of nearby homes and fencing
  • Branch and log processing
  • Mechanical loading and haul-off
  • Final driveway and work-area cleanup
