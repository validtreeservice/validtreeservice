The job from start to finish

A controlled plan for a large tree close to the home.

Tree work near roofs, driveways and occupied areas requires more than simply making cuts. The crew must choose safe tie-in points, establish a clear work zone and control how each section moves. This Baytown project documents that process with real photographs and footage from the property.

Property protection

Ropes and controlled rigging helped manage tree sections above and beside the residence. The climber and ground crew communicated throughout the work so cut material could be guided into the established landing area.

Crew and equipment

Hardhats, climbing equipment, rigging lines and organized ground support were visible throughout the project. Limbs and trunk sections were processed and loaded instead of being left scattered across the property.

Complete cleanup

The scope included debris haul-off, stump grinding and detailed ground cleanup. Final photographs show the opened canopy, cleared work area and finished residential property.
