Residential project

Careful access and cleanup around backyard structures.

The property contained several mature trees and multiple obstacles, including a shed, covered patio and fence lines. The work plan had to account for limited access while keeping cut material organized and protecting the areas the homeowner would continue using after the crew left.

Controlled cutting

The crew cut and processed tree sections around the home and backyard improvements. Working in planned stages kept branches and trunk wood from collecting against the shed, patio and fencing.

Compact equipment

A mini loader moved heavier material through the work area and supported efficient debris processing. Equipment was selected to help move tree sections while working within the residential access available.

Haul-off and finish

Cut material was consolidated and removed from the property. The final sequence shows cleared fence lines, cleaned work areas and an open backyard after the multi-tree project was completed.
