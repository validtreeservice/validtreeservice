Questions before you call

Clear answers for Houston property owners.

Are you insured?

Yes. Valid Tree Service is insured, and proof of insurance can be provided when requested for your project.

Do you remove and haul away debris?

Haul-off and cleanup can be included in the written scope. Your estimate will clearly state what material is removed, chipped, stacked, or left onsite.

Can I text photos for an estimate?

Yes. Text photos, the property address, and a short description to 832-445-6535. Some projects still require an onsite inspection before final pricing.

Do you work around homes, fences and utility lines?

We regularly plan controlled tree work around structures and tight access. Each property is evaluated so the appropriate crew, rigging and equipment can be used.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Houston and surrounding communities, including Pasadena, Baytown, Crosby, Spring, Tomball, Conroe, Liberty, Hockley and nearby areas.

Do you handle storm-damaged trees?

Yes. Call or text us with photos and the address. If a tree is touching a power line, contact the utility provider and emergency services as appropriate before approaching it.
