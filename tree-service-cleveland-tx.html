Homes, lots & acreage

Choose the work your property needs.

We review tree condition, nearby buildings, fences, access and your plans for the land before defining the scope.

Tree removal, including large & hazardous trees

Dead, damaged or unwanted trees may need controlled sectional removal around a home or outbuilding. We assess room for cutting, rigging and moving material, then explain the removal and cleanup plan. Send wide photos showing the whole tree and what surrounds it.

Tree trimming & limb removal

Selective trimming can address limbs over roofs, fences and access routes. Tell us which branches concern you and what clearance you need. The estimate should identify the intended cuts and how branches will be chipped, stacked or hauled away.

Stump grinding & root-ball removal

Grinding an old stump and excavating a root ball are different jobs. We review your intended use of the area, stump size and equipment access to quote the appropriate work. Confirm grinding depth, chip handling, root removal and any backfill requirements in the written scope.

Land, lot & brush clearing

For wooded lots and acreage, mark the area to clear and the trees to retain. We can quote brush and tree removal, material handling and haul-off for the agreed area. Tell us whether the goal is usable yard space, access or vegetation clearing ahead of construction.

Fence-line clearing

Brush and trees can block fence repairs or access along a property edge. Show us the confirmed boundary, approximate length and access points. We define the clearing width and debris plan with you before work begins.

Storm cleanup & debris haul-off

We review fallen limbs, damaged trees and heavy woody debris for removal and cleanup. Call with the address and explain any blocked access. Keep away from trees touching power lines and contact the utility provider before approaching them.

From first call to written scope

Get a useful estimate.

  1. Share the property. Call or send the address, clear photos, approximate tree size or acreage, and your timeline.
  2. Show the access. Include gates, driveways, slopes, nearby structures and overhead lines. For clearing, mark what stays and what goes.
  3. Review the scope. We determine whether an onsite visit is needed and specify removal, stump or root work, material handling and cleanup before scheduling.

Cleveland tree service questions.

Can I get a quote from photos?

Photos help us review the work and access. Large trees, limited access and clearing projects may need a site visit before final pricing. Call or text 832-445-6535 to start.

Do you handle both residential and commercial work?

Yes. We quote residential tree removal, trimming and stump grinding as well as land clearing, commercial clearing and subcontract vegetation-removal scopes. Each project is reviewed for access, equipment and scheduling.

Is haul-off or stump grinding included?

Only when it is listed in your estimate. Ask for stump grinding, root-ball removal, chipping, haul-off and the desired site finish to be identified separately so the scope matches your plans.

Do you still serve Houston?

Yes. Cleveland and nearby communities are our primary local focus, and Houston remains an important service area. Our established Houston service pages and project galleries remain available.
